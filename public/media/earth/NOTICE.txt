Changes made locally for First Light:
- earth.glb embeds the original BIN and original JPEG in a self-contained GLB.
  JSON is minified and resources receive GLB alignment padding. All original
  geometry bytes, image bytes, scene graph, names, materials and coordinates
  are retained. No geometry optimization, compression, index conversion or
  transform baking was performed.
- earth-icon.glb is a separately named UI derivative. Its geometry, UVs,
  materials, names and coordinates are unchanged. Only its texture is reduced
  from 8192x4096 to 1024x512 with Pillow LANCZOS and JPEG quality 90 (4:2:0).
  The prepared JPEG is in icon/Scene_-_Root_baseColor.jpeg. It is not claimed
  to be byte-identical to the original JPEG. No generative imaging, automatic
  color transform or image enhancement was used.
- scripts/pack-moon-glb.mjs is the existing local single-BIN/JPEG packer,
  reused unchanged despite its historical name. It is not a newly supplied
  Earth converter. scripts/prepare-earth-icon.py records the derivative build.

See docs/earth-asset-provenance.md and docs/earth-glb-validation.json in the
repository for hashes, exact byte sizes, validation and reproduction commands.
